Key ledger

Every key and keycard Key Cache knows, with its icon, the short name the game prints on it in your stash, and its number. Use it to turn a screenshot of your keys into an import code, by hand or by giving this page (or ledger.md / keys.json) to an AI along with the screenshot.

Make an import code

  1. Match each key in the screenshot to a card below by its short name (top-right of the icon, as in the stash) and its icon.
  2. Write KC: and then the numbers of the matching keys, separated by commas: . That's the whole code, no encoding needed.
  3. On the site open Menu › Back up or move your keys, paste it and press Add to my keys, or Restore to replace your current keys.

Good to know

  • The import box also takes a plain list of numbers, ids, full key names or short names, one per line or separated by commas. Old base64 codes still work.
  • Numbers are the safest. Cards marked Shared name share a short name (or full name) with another key, so check the icon and use the number.
  • Click a number to copy it. For an AI, keys.txt is the smallest list to paste in.
